Three regimes apply to your purchase. They do not replace one another: they add up.

1. Right of withdrawal — 14 days

You have fourteen days to change your mind, with no reason to give and no penalty. In the European Union this right comes from Directive 2011/83/EU; the United Kingdom applies the same fourteen-day period under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. Buyers in the United States, Canada and Australia are covered by the same commercial policy, applied voluntarily, on top of their own statutory rights.

When the clock starts: the day you, or a third party you designated, physically take possession of the last product in the order. If the deadline falls on a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday, it runs to the next working day.

How to exercise it: send an unambiguous statement to [TO BE COMPLETED] before the end of the fourteenth day, with your order number, the products concerned and the delivery date. A model form is available on request; using it is not compulsory.

Sending it back: you then have fourteen days to return the product to [TO BE COMPLETED]. Return shipping is at your expense: [TO BE COMPLETED].

Refund: within fourteen days of receiving the return, or of proof of its dispatch if that reaches us first. It includes the outbound delivery cost at the standard rate; the extra cost of an express delivery stays with you.

2. Commercial guarantee — 30 nights

A pillow cannot be judged in one night. The 30-night guarantee gives you time to try it at home. Terms, products covered and procedure: 30-night guarantee.

This is a commercial guarantee, granted freely by PORTANCE. It adds to the right of withdrawal and the statutory guarantees below. It does not replace them, does not limit them and does not make them conditional.

3. Statutory guarantees

Guarantee of conformity — 2 years (EU and UK)

We are liable for lack of conformity for two years from delivery, under Directive (EU) 2019/771. Any defect appearing within 24 months is presumed to have existed on the day of delivery: you do not have to prove it. You can ask for repair or replacement; failing that, a price reduction or cancellation of the sale. In the United Kingdom the Consumer Rights Act 2015 gives equivalent remedies, including a short-term right to reject within 30 days.

Hidden defects and other national rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may also have a separate action for hidden defects, and additional consumer guarantees — for example the Australian Consumer Law guarantees, or state and provincial warranty law in the United States and Canada. Nothing on this page limits those rights.

These guarantees are free and depend on no commercial condition: no kept packaging, no product registration, no purchase of an option.

What cannot be taken back

  • Sleep strips and nasal strips: sealed hygiene products. Once the seal is broken they are neither taken back nor exchanged. With the seal intact, the right of withdrawal applies normally.
  • Pillowcases: taken back unwashed, unused, in their original packaging.
  • A product handled beyond what is necessary to establish its nature, characteristics and proper working: you may be liable for any diminished value. Trying a pillow at home is normal; staining it is not.

Before breaking the seal on a box of sleep strips, check the contraindications: undiagnosed or untreated sleep apnoea, nasal obstruction, a cold, respiratory or cardiac conditions, alcohol or sedatives, nausea or reflux, children, pregnancy. If in doubt, leave the seal closed: the box stays returnable.
