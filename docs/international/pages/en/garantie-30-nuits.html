A pillow is judged when you wake up, not in a shop

Your hand knows nothing about a pillow. Your body takes a few nights to decide. That is why both our pillows, Appui and Aplomb, come with a 30-night commercial guarantee: you sleep on it at home, and you decide afterwards.

What the 30-night guarantee covers

  • A home trial of the Appui and Aplomb pillows, in your own bed, on your real nights.
  • A refund of the product price if the pillow isn't right for you, with no need to justify your decision.

It applies to the pillows only. Pillowcases and strips are excluded: see below.

Length and starting point

30 nights from the day your order arrives, not from the order or dispatch date. Your request must reach us by the 30th day after delivery at the latest.

What condition the pillow must come back in

It has been used: that is the whole point of a trial, and normal signs of use are no problem at all. We ask that it comes back:

  • complete, with its cover and, for the Appui, its cotton pillowcase;
  • clean and dry, with no marked stain or lingering smell;
  • with no tear, burn or escaped filling;
  • in packaging that protects it in transit.

Refund time

We refund within 14 days of receiving the return, to the payment method used for the order.

What is excluded

Sleep strips and nasal strips are neither taken back nor exchanged once the packaging is open. They are sealed hygiene products, worn on the lips or on the nose, so the hygiene exception to the right of withdrawal applies. While the seal is intact, a return is still possible under the 14-day right of withdrawal.

Also excluded: pillowcases that have been washed or used, and damage caused by abnormal use (machine-washing the filling, chemicals, cutting, pets).

A reminder: sleep strips are not suitable in cases of undiagnosed or untreated sleep apnoea, nasal obstruction, a cold, respiratory or cardiac conditions, alcohol or sedatives, nausea or reflux. They are not intended for children or pregnant women. They are not medical devices.

What the 30-night guarantee is not

It is a commercial guarantee, granted freely by PORTANCE. It adds to your statutory rights and never replaces them. Independently of it, you keep:

  • the 14-day right of withdrawal — in the EU under Directive 2011/83/EU, in the UK under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 — from delivery, with no reason to give;
  • the 2-year guarantee of conformity under Directive (EU) 2019/771, or the equivalent remedies of the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015, which cover lack of conformity;
  • any further national consumer rights — for example an action for hidden defects, the Australian Consumer Law guarantees, or state and provincial warranty law in the United States and Canada.

In practice: on day 8, your right of withdrawal applies. On day 25, the 30-night guarantee. If a seam gives way in month 14, the statutory guarantee of conformity — and it costs you nothing.
