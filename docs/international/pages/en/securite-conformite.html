Our products spend the night against your face. This page says what they comply with, what they are not, and what you need to check at your end.

What our products are not

No product in the range is a medical device. Not the pillows, not the pillowcases, not the sleep strips, not the nasal strips. They do not cure, treat or prevent any condition — in particular not sleep apnoea, pathological snoring or any breathing disorder. Their effect is mechanical and a matter of comfort.

If you have a sleep or breathing disorder, the person to talk to is a doctor. A pillow is not a treatment, and an online shop is not a clinic.

Before first use

  • Pillows: take the pillow out of its compressed packaging and let it recover its volume for 24 hours, laid flat in a ventilated room. A neutral new-foam smell may linger for a few days; it goes with airing.
  • Pillowcases and covers: wash before first use, at 30 °C, delicate cycle.
  • Strips: before a first full night, test the strip for an hour while awake, on clean, dry skin with no cream on it.

The five rules that actually matter

  1. Respect the contraindications for the strips. Do not use sleep strips in cases of undiagnosed or untreated sleep apnoea, nasal obstruction, a cold, a respiratory or cardiac condition, alcohol or sedatives, nausea or reflux. They are not intended for children or pregnant women.
  2. You must be able to remove them instantly. A sleep strip comes off in one movement. If it doesn't, don't use it.
  3. Never machine-wash the filling. Only the cover and the pillowcase are washable. A filling put through a machine is beyond saving, and the guarantee does not cover it.
  4. Air them; do not tumble dry. No tumble dryer, no radiator, no direct sun: heat degrades foams and fibres.
  5. A pillow is not a nursery item. Never put a pillow in an infant's bed: risk of suffocation. Paediatric guidance advises against pillows before the age of two.

What not to do

  • Don't cut a strip to resize it: the existing central cut-out is dimensioned to let air through.
  • Don't reuse a single-use strip.
  • Don't apply a strip to broken, irritated, freshly shaved or cream-covered skin.
  • Don't use a pillow whose cover is torn or whose filling is escaping.
  • Don't expose the products to a prolonged heat source.

Skin reactions

If you get redness, itching, stinging or a burning sensation, remove the strip and stop using it. If the reaction lasts more than a few hours, see a healthcare professional.
