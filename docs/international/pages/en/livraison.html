Parcel damaged on arrival

  • Inspect the parcel in front of the courier where you can.
  • Write precise, dated reservations on the delivery note or in the carrier's app: “box crushed on the right-hand corner, cover torn”. A generic “subject to unpacking” carries no weight.
  • Photograph the sealed parcel, the label, the packing material, then the product.
  • Write to us within 3 working days with those photos.

We replace or refund. You do not have to negotiate with the carrier.

Parcel lost or never delivered

If tracking says “delivered” and you have nothing: check the letterbox, the pickup point and your neighbours, then write to us. We open an investigation with the carrier after [TO BE COMPLETED] working days without delivery. The investigation takes [TO BE COMPLETED]. At the end of it, you choose a reshipment or a refund.

Where no delivery date has been agreed, delivery takes place within 30 days of the order at the latest. Past that point you can require us to deliver, then cancel the sale and be refunded. In the European Union this follows Directive 2011/83/EU; equivalent rights apply in the United Kingdom, and consumer protection legislation in the United States, Canada and Australia gives you comparable remedies.

Wrong address

An incomplete address stops a parcel. If you spot the mistake before the dispatch notice, write to us: we correct it. After dispatch, the address can no longer be changed. A parcel returned for a wrong address is re-sent once new shipping charges are paid ([TO BE COMPLETED]), or refunded less the original shipping cost.

Customs and taxes outside your own customs union

Prices are shown in your local currency. For deliveries inside the European Union, EU VAT is included. For a destination outside the customs union the goods ship from, you are the importer: customs duties, local VAT or sales tax and the carrier's handling fee are payable by you and are claimed on delivery. We can neither estimate nor advance them. A parcel refused at customs comes back to us: the costs incurred are not refunded.
