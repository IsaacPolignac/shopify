Bedding is sold in an aisle. You sleep at home.

A pillow gets bought in three seconds, under white light, by pressing it with the flat of your hand. That gesture tells you nothing about what happens six hours later, once your neck has carried the weight of your head all night. In the aisle, the sale turns on appearance: the white of the cotton, the thickness, the logo on the cover. Rest happens somewhere else entirely.

PORTANCE came out of that gap. We don't start from a package or a material trend, but from one simple and rarely used fact: the position you actually sleep in. On your side, a gap stays open between your ear and the mattress, and that gap has a height. A flat pillow doesn't fill it. Too thick a pillow overshoots it. Either way, the body compensates for hours.

The name

Portance is the French word for lift — in mechanics, the force that holds a mass in the air. Nothing romantic about it: a force, a direction, an intensity. It is exactly what you want from a pillow, and it is rarely how pillows get talked about.

We are not selling a softness record. We are selling a night that goes better than the last one, because your neck, your shoulder and your hip were held in the right place for seven hours.

The name commits us to a rule: if a product adds a constraint instead of removing one, it has no place in the range. Our line fits in four words — what holds you. By design: shape, height, density, breathability. Not by promise.

What we refuse to do

The sleep market talks a lot. Three practices are routine in it, and we will have none of them.

  • No medical promises. Our products do not cure, do not treat and do not prevent anything. A contoured pillow supports a neck and a shoulder: that is a mechanical effect and a matter of comfort, not a treatment. Our sleep strips and nasal strips are not medical devices. If you have a sleep or breathing disorder, the person to talk to is a doctor, not an online shop.
  • No manufactured reviews. No invented average rating, no testimonial written by us, no percentage of satisfied customers. We have just opened: we have no history yet, and we would rather write it than paper over it.
  • No false urgency. No countdown that restarts on every visit, no “only three left” shown by default. When a price is struck through, it refers to a real reference price. When we state a delivery time, it is one we know we can meet.

How a product gets into the range

One question only: does it help you sleep? If the answer rests on a selling point rather than on something observable in a bed, the product doesn't make it. Hence a range of seven items, not sixty.

  • The Appui Pillow, contoured, three support zones: neck, shoulder, hip. High-resilience fibre, 60 × 40 cm, 12 cm high, removable cotton cover washable at 30 °C, cotton pillowcase included.
  • The Aplomb Pillow, perforated and ventilated memory foam, twin bolster, breathable knit cover, 60 × 40 cm, 10/12 cm high.
  • The pillowcases, treated as products in their own right: technical heat-dissipating knit, or 200-thread combed cotton.
  • The sleep strips and nasal strips, two strictly mechanical accessories: the first go on the lips, with an open central cut-out, to encourage nasal breathing; the second are spring strips that hold the nostrils open. Their contraindications are on every product page: undiagnosed or untreated apnoea, nasal obstruction, a cold, respiratory or cardiac conditions, alcohol or sedatives, nausea or reflux, children, pregnancy.

What we still have to prove

We are a young brand, and it shows. We have no multi-season feedback, no measurement of how our fillings hold up after two years of nights, no shop where you can try a pillow before buying it.

We make up for it the only honest way we know: by giving the numbers before the purchase — dimensions, heights, materials, washing temperatures — and by letting you try at home. Both our pillows carry a 30-night commercial guarantee, which adds to your 14-day right of withdrawal and your 2-year statutory guarantee of conformity, without ever replacing them.

The rest gets built as you sleep on them. We will update this page when we have learned something worth putting on it.
