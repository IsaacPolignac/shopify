30-night guarantee

The Appui and Aplomb pillows can be tried for thirty nights at home. If they aren't right, one email is enough and we refund the product price. This commercial guarantee adds to your statutory rights and never replaces them.

Statutory guarantees

A two-year guarantee of conformity from delivery, plus any further national consumer rights — an action for hidden defects, the Australian Consumer Law guarantees, or state and provincial warranty law in the United States and Canada. They are free and depend on no commercial condition: no kept packaging, no product registration, no purchase of an option.

What cannot be taken back

Sleep strips and nasal strips are sealed hygiene products: once the seal is opened they are neither taken back nor exchanged. With the seal intact, the right of withdrawal applies normally. Pillowcases must come back unwashed and unused, in their original packaging.
