Until the parcel is physically handed over to you, the risk of loss or damage is ours. Where no delivery date has been agreed, delivery takes place within thirty days of the order at the latest.

Parcel damaged or lost

Write precise reservations for the carrier at the moment of handover, photograph the parcel and the product, then write to us within three working days. Opening the claim is our job, not yours: we are the sender.

Customs outside the shipping zone

For a destination outside the customs union the goods ship from, you are the importer: duties, local taxes and the carrier's handling fee are payable by you and are claimed on delivery. We can neither estimate nor advance them.
